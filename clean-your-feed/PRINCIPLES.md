# A Biblical Foundation for Your Feed

[← Back to the guide](README.md)

> "And we all, with unveiled face, beholding the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another."
> — 2 Corinthians 3:18

**We become like what we behold.** Scripture teaches that what we look at and dwell on shapes who we become. Social media feeds are built to hold our attention, so they shape us too, whether we notice it or not. Cleaning your feed isn't about rules or earning God's favor. It's about choosing on purpose what will shape your heart.

> "There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus." — Romans 8:1

If your feed has been a struggle, start here: God's grace meets you right where you are. This is a fresh start, not a guilt trip.

> "The steadfast love of the LORD never ceases; his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning; great is your faithfulness." — Lamentations 3:22–23

## Seven principles

### 1. Guard your heart and your eyes

> "Keep your heart with all vigilance, for from it flow the springs of life." — Proverbs 4:23
>
> "I will not set before my eyes anything that is worthless." — Psalm 101:3

Your feed is a doorway into your heart. You would lock a door at home. In the same way, you can decide what comes through your screen.

**In practice:** Unfollow, mute, and tap "Not interested" on anything that pulls you toward impurity, cynicism, or envy. Use keyword filters and sensitive-content settings. *(Step 1 in every guide)*

### 2. Fill your mind with what is good

> "Finally, brothers, whatever is true, whatever is honorable, whatever is just, whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, whatever is commendable, if there is any excellence, if there is anything worthy of praise, think about these things." — Philippians 4:8

Removing bad content leaves a gap. Fill it on purpose with what is true, lovely, and praiseworthy.

**In practice:** Use Philippians 4:8 as a filter. Before you follow, like, or share, ask: *Is this true? Honorable? Pure? Lovely?* Follow accounts that point you toward God, your family, your health, and learning.

### 3. Choose your company wisely

> "Whoever walks with the wise becomes wise, but the companion of fools will suffer harm." — Proverbs 13:20
>
> "Do not be deceived: 'Bad company ruins good morals.'" — 1 Corinthians 15:33

Every account you follow is a voice you've invited to speak into your life every day. Would you choose these voices as friends?

**In practice:** Go through your following list slowly. Keep the voices that build faith, hope, and love. Release the rest without guilt.

### 4. Make the best use of your time

> "Look carefully then how you walk, not as unwise but as wise, making the best use of the time, because the days are evil." — Ephesians 5:15–16
>
> "So teach us to number our days that we may get a heart of wisdom." — Psalm 90:12

Time is a gift from God and we are its stewards. Endless scrolling quietly spends hours that could go to prayer, people, rest, and service.

**In practice:** Set daily time limits and break reminders. Turn off notifications that pull you back in. *(Step 4 "Set guardrails" in every guide)*

### 5. Don't be mastered by anything

> "'All things are lawful for me,' but not all things are helpful. 'All things are lawful for me,' but I will not be dominated by anything." — 1 Corinthians 6:12
>
> "Let us also lay aside every weight, and sin which clings so closely, and let us run with endurance the race that is set before us." — Hebrews 12:1

Some content isn't sinful, but it's still a *weight*. If an app decides when you pick up your phone, it has become your master.

**In practice:** Notice when you reach for your phone without deciding to. Move tempting apps off your home screen. Take regular breaks, and plan a weekly digital Sabbath (see principle 7).

### 6. Find your identity in Christ, not in comparison

> "But when they measure themselves by one another and compare themselves with one another, they are without understanding." — 2 Corinthians 10:12
>
> "For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, that we should walk in them." — Ephesians 2:10
>
> "Set your minds on things that are above, not on things that are on earth. For you have died, and your life is hidden with Christ in God." — Colossians 3:2–3

Feeds show everyone else's highlight reel. Comparison breeds envy, discontent, and anxiety. Your worth was settled at the cross, not by likes or followers.

**In practice:** Unfollow accounts that leave you feeling "less than." When anxiety rises, pray instead of scroll:

> "Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus." — Philippians 4:6–7

### 7. Rest, and be still

> "Be still, and know that I am God." — Psalm 46:10
>
> "Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy." — Exodus 20:8
>
> "And he said to them, 'Come away by yourselves to a desolate place and rest a while.'" — Mark 6:31

God built rest into creation itself. The Sabbath is a weekly invitation to step away from the noise and be with Him, with family, and in nature.

**In practice:**

- **Keep a digital Sabbath.** Set notifications and feeds aside from sunset to sunset each week, and use the time for worship, family, and the outdoors.
- **Guard your sleep.** Use the bedtime and sleep-mode settings, and charge your phone outside the bedroom. *"In peace I will both lie down and sleep; for you alone, O LORD, make me dwell in safety." — Psalm 4:8*
- **Start the day with God, not the feed.** Open the Word before you open an app.

## Using social media for good

Social media isn't evil. It's a tool, and tools can serve God's purposes.

> "In the same way, let your light shine before others, so that they may see your good works and give glory to your Father who is in heaven." — Matthew 5:16
>
> "Let no corrupting talk come out of your mouths, but only such as is good for building up, as fits the occasion, that it may give grace to those who hear." — Ephesians 4:29

- **Post and comment to build up.** Encourage, give thanks, and share hope.
- **Be slow to engage in arguments.** *"Let every person be quick to hear, slow to speak, slow to anger." — James 1:19*
- **Don't spread gossip or outrage.** *"For lack of wood the fire goes out, and where there is no whisperer, quarreling ceases." — Proverbs 26:20*
- **Let it point you toward real community.** *"Let us consider how to stir up one another to love and good works, not neglecting to meet together." — Hebrews 10:24–25*

## A 7-day Feed Renewal plan

Read the verse each day, pray, then take the action.

| Day | Read | Pray | Do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Psalm 139:23–24 | "Search me, O God… see if there be any grievous way in me." | Notice how you feel after scrolling each app. Write it down. |
| 2 | Proverbs 4:23; Psalm 101:3 | Ask God to show you what you've been letting in. | **Remove the noise:** unfollow or mute 10 accounts. |
| 3 | Philippians 4:8 | Thank God for what is good, true, and lovely. | **Follow what builds:** follow 5 encouraging accounts. |
| 4 | Lamentations 3:22–23 | Receive His new-every-morning mercy. | **Reset:** clear YouTube history, reset Instagram suggestions, refresh TikTok. |
| 5 | Ephesians 5:15–16 | Offer your time to God. | **Set guardrails:** time limits, break reminders, notifications off. |
| 6 | Exodus 20:8–11; Mark 6:31 | Ask for true rest. | **Plan your digital Sabbath**, and charge your phone outside the bedroom. |
| 7 | Colossians 3:1–3 | Thank God that your life is hidden with Christ. | **Keep it in perspective:** spend the time you saved with God and people. |

## For small groups and families

1. Which app, account, or habit most affects your mood or your thoughts? How?
2. Read Philippians 4:8. Which of the eight qualities is hardest to find in your feed?
3. How does comparison online affect how you see yourself and others? Read 2 Corinthians 10:12 and Ephesians 2:10.
4. What would change if you gave God the first 15 minutes of your day instead of your phone?
5. What could a digital Sabbath look like for your household?
6. How could you use your own posts to "let your light shine" (Matthew 5:16)?

## A prayer

> Lord, You made my mind and You care about what fills it. Forgive me for the times I've let my feed shape me more than Your Word. Give me wisdom to choose what I watch, follow, and share. Guard my heart and my eyes. Teach me to number my days and use my time well. Free me from comparison, and remind me that my life is hidden with Christ in You. Renew my mind, so that I may know Your good, pleasing, and perfect will. In Jesus' name, amen.
