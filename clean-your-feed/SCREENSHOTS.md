# Swapping in real screenshots

The guides currently use annotated **illustrations** (`.svg`). To replace any of them with a real screenshot:

1. Take the screenshot listed below on your phone.
2. **Before saving, check for privacy:** crop out or blur other people's names, faces, profile photos, and messages, plus your own notifications, battery, and clock if you like. Use a test or family account when you can.
3. *(Optional)* Mark it up in your phone's photo editor: draw a red circle around the button and add a number that matches the step in the guide.
4. Save it with the **same file name** but a `.png` extension, in the same folder. For example, save `images/youtube/02-clear-pause-history.png` next to the existing `.svg`.
5. In the guide, change that one image link from `.svg` to `.png`.
6. Once every screenshot for a platform is in, you can delete that folder's `.svg` files.

Aim for **portrait phone screenshots** of about 1080 px wide. The guides display them 300 px wide.

## Why not just use screenshots from the internet?

Screenshots of these apps are copyrighted by their publishers or by whoever took them, and they often show other people's accounts. Taking your own avoids both problems.
